
# product noodles
Here's the cheapest possible end-to-end flow — you can do the whole thing for **NT$0/month** if you already own a phone/Mac.

**The economic stack (all free):**

1. **Record** — Your iPhone (Voice Memos) or Mac. A phone in a quiet, soft-furnished room beats a cheap USB mic in a bare room. Cost: $0.
2. **Edit** — **Audacity** (free, macOS) or **GarageBand** (free, already on your Mac). Trim, remove noise, normalize levels. Cost: $0.
3. **Host + Distribute** — **Spotify for Creators** (formerly Anchor). Unlimited free hosting, unlimited episodes, no fees, and it generates an RSS feed that pushes to Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, etc. This is the single most generous free host in 2026. Cost: $0.
4. **Publish to Apple Podcasts** — submit that same RSS feed once in Apple Podcasts Connect. Cost: $0.

**Taiwan-specific tip:** Since your content is Chinese/Taiwan-focused, **Firstory** is the local heavyweight — free unlimited storage and downloads, with analytics and promo tools, and it's what most Taiwanese podcasters (親子天下, 從前從前, etc.) actually use. It distributes to Apple/Spotify/KKBOX and handles the local discovery ecosystem better than Spotify for Creators alone.

**The full flow in one line:**  
Record (phone/Mac) → edit (Audacity/GarageBand) → upload to Firstory _or_ Spotify for Creators → they auto-generate RSS → distributes to all major apps → done.

**Two honest cost caveats:**

- The one upgrade genuinely worth money later is a **USB mic** (~NT$2,000–3,500, one-time) — it's the biggest quality jump per dollar. Not needed to start.
- "Free" hosts monetize via their own ecosystem (Spotify takes 50% of ad revenue if you ever join their partner program). Fine when you're starting; irrelevant until you have an audience.

For your situation specifically — solo, Taiwan, Chinese content — I'd go **Firstory for hosting + GarageBand for editing + iPhone for recording**. Zero cost, native-quality Mandarin distribution, and the path most local creators take.
