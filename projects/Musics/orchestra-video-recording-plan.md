# Orchestra Contest Video Recording Plan

**Goal:** Record Serena's orchestra contest (upright bass), 2 pieces, ≤ 15 min.
**Decision:** Rent **Sony AX700 + Manfrotto 190XPROB / 496RC2** from [RentLens](https://www.rentlens.com.tw). Use the camera's built-in mic.

## Why not the Nikon P1000
- Tiny 1/2.3" sensor, and the aperture closes to f/8 when zoomed in, so footage is noisy on a stage.
- Very shaky past ~600–800mm; poor built-in audio; zoom motor noise gets recorded.

- **Z190** only makes sense if seated far back and you need a tight close-up.
- **AX700** reach is fine from front/middle seats; use Clear Image Zoom for a bit more.

## Audio
- **Skip the DJI Mic** (NT$400). It records directly into the video and stays in sync, but the transmitter has to be near the stage (unlikely to be allowed in a contest) and it's designed for voices.
- Use the **AX700 built-in stereo mic**.
- Optional backup: record on a phone, then sync by waveform in Final Cut Pro / DaVinci Resolve.

## Budget
- AX700 NT$1,100 + tripod NT$200 = **NT$1,300/day**
- Deposits: NT$5,000 + NT$2,000 = **NT$7,000**
- Ask whether a **spare battery** and an **SD card (V30+)** are included.

## Checklist
- [ ] Confirm the venue allows filming + tripods
- [ ] Book AX700 + 190XPROB/496RC2
- [ ] Pick up a day early; practice at home
- [ ] Settings: Auto mode, 4K30 (or 1080p), SteadyShot on
- [ ] Charge batteries; format the SD card in the camera
- [ ] At the hall: level the tripod, tighten the ball head firmly
- [ ] Frame Serena + bass section, touch to focus
- [ ] Start recording a little early; **don't touch it** until the music ends
- [ ] (Optional) Phone audio backup
