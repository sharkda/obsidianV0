**1. Rehearsal K: Meno Mosso, ♩ = 120, "Floating, in 2"**

- "Meno mosso" means "less motion," so the music slows down.
- "In 2" means the conductor beats **2 per bar**, one for each half note, instead of 4. That makes each beat feel slow (60 per minute), which is the floating feeling. You mostly have long notes here, so feel the half-note beat.
- "(in 4)" in bar 76 means the conductor goes back to 4 quarter-note beats. That gets you ready for the faster tempo coming next.

**2. Rehearsal L: Agitato, ♩ = 148, "Turbulence"**

- "Agitato" means agitated. The tempo jumps from 120 to 148.
- From bar 80 you get bars of **7/8 (2+2+3)** and **5/8 (3+2)** mixed in with normal bars.
- **The one rule to remember: the eighth note always stays the same speed.**
    - A group of 2 eighths = one normal quarter-note beat.
    - A group of 3 eighths = a **longer** beat, 1½ quarters (a dotted quarter).
    - So **7/8 (2+2+3)** feels like _beat, beat, LONG beat_: "1-2, 1-2, 1-2-3."
    - **5/8 (3+2)** feels like _LONG beat, beat_: "1-2-3, 1-2."
- The conductor beats 3 in the 7/8 bars (the last beat is longer) and 2 in the 5/8 bars (the first beat is longer).
- Your pencil counts (1234 56 7, 123) are correct. Counting eighths and grouping them 2+2+3 or 3+2 is the right method.

**How to practice**
