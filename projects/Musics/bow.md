“**2. Where the upgrade can come from**

- **Carbon fiber (碳纖維弓)**: the best choice for a backup. It’s durable and won’t warp in Taiwan’s humidity, it feels consistent, and it usually responds better than an entry-level Brazilwood (巴西木) bow. A wooden bow at a higher level costs much more, and the difference isn’t worth it on a fractional bass.
- **Weight and balance**: similar to her current bow or slightly lighter. Have her try it; a well-balanced bow makes bouncing and string-crossing passages easier.
- **Fittings**: an ebony frog (烏木弓根) and solid winding and screw are signs of decent quality.”
